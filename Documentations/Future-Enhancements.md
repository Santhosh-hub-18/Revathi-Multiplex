# Future Enhancements

## 1. Overview

The Revathi Multiplex application can be enhanced with additional features in future versions.

## 2. Online Payment Integration

Integrate an online payment gateway to allow customers to complete ticket payments directly through the application.

## 3. Mobile Application

Develop a mobile application so customers can book movie tickets using smartphones.

## 4. Seat Selection

Allow customers to view the theatre seating layout and select specific seats during the booking process.

## 5. Booking History

Provide customers with access to their previous and upcoming movie bookings.

## 6. Cancellation and Refund

Implement automated ticket cancellation and refund processing.

## 7. Reports and Dashboards

Provide dashboards and reports for:

- Total bookings
- Completed bookings
- Cancelled bookings
- Revenue
- Movie popularity
- Show performance

## 8. Automated Notifications

Extend notifications to support:

- Email
- SMS
- Mobile push notifications

## 9. Customer Feedback

Allow customers to provide ratings and feedback after watching a movie.

## 10. Conclusion

These enhancements can improve the customer experience and make the Revathi Multiplex application more scalable and feature-rich.
